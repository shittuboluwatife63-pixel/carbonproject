    Biometric Data (Face Data)

    As part of our identity verification ("Know Your Customer" or "KYC") process, we use your device's camera, including the TrueDepth camera on supported devices, to capture a live facial image and/or short video ("face data"). This is used to: (i) perform liveness detection, confirming you are physically present and not using a photograph, replayed video, mask, or other spoofing method; and (ii) compare your live facial image with the photograph on the government-issued identification document you submit. Face data is collected and processed solely for identity verification and compliance with applicable KYC, Anti-Money Laundering (AML), and other regulatory requirements issued by the Central Bank of Nigeria (CBN) during customer onboarding and, where applicable, verification of directors and beneficial owners.To facilitate this, we share face data only with our contracted identity verification provider ("the Provider"), which processes it on our behalf to perform liveness detection and biometric matching. The Provider shall use face data solely for this purpose and is prohibited from using it for any other. Verification records, including facial images where required, are securely stored by us and may also be securely retained by the Provider in connection with the provision of the service. Face data is never sold or disclosed to any other third party, except as required by law or as necessary to provide the identity verification services described above. Face data is not used for advertising, marketing, behavioural profiling, analytics, or any purpose unrelated to identity verification. We retain face data and related verification records only for as long as necessary to meet applicable legal, regulatory, and record-retention obligations.
